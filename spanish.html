Reading guide: "Desayunos exquisitos"

Summarized in English, organized the way the reading is. Read the real text first, then use this to check you understood it.

Intro: Breakfasts vary across the Spanish-speaking world (coffee, chocolate, mate, eggs, fruit, cheese, even rice and broth). Many doctors call it the most important meal of the day.

Las bebidas:

café"king" of breakfast drinks. Drunk almost everywhere, e.g. Spain, Colombia, Costa Rica, and Cuba, where it is very sweet.
chocolatePopular in Mexico, Colombia, Guatemala. Dark or with milk.
mateArgentina, Paraguay, Uruguay.
atoleMexico. A pre-Hispanic drink of corn cooked in water with spices like cinnamon and vanilla.

La harina: if coffee is the king, flour (wheat or corn) is the "queen" of breakfast.

panEaten everywhere.
arepaA must in Colombia and Venezuela. A corn cake, plain, topped with butter or cheese, or stuffed.
pupusas / gorditasCentral America and Mexico. Similar to the arepa.
tamalesMexico is their "cradle." Corn dough, stuffed and wrapped in leaves. Sweet or savory. Also popular in Central America, the Caribbean, parts of South America, and even Miami.

Otras opciones:

Ecuador (coast)Fish and seafood.
El SalvadorGround and fried beans (frijoles).
Nicaragua, Costa Ricagallo pinto, rice and beans. Same dish as cultura #24!
Colombia (cold areas)caldo de costilla, a rib broth. Some eat changua, a broth of milk, egg, cheese and cilantro.

Practice: Cierto o Falso

These are practice statements, not the ones in the homework. Decide, and if it's false, say how to fix it. Then check.

How to fix a false one

  • Find the paragraph the statement is about: Las bebidas, La harina, or Otras opciones.
  • Rewrite the statement so it matches the reading, changing only the wrong part.
  • Example: Falso: El mate es popular en México. → El mate es popular en Argentina, Paraguay y Uruguay.
